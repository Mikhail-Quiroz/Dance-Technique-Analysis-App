# High Quality - Fan Kick vs Front Battement

Front battements and fan kicks, both filmed facing the camera. From the front the app mixes them
up: the 6 front-facing front battements in "High Quality - Front Battement" (clips 9-14) all read
as fan kicks or side battements. This folder is for telling the two apart.

Filming them in blocks makes the key easy, for example 3 front battements on each leg, then 3 fan
kicks on each leg. Any order works: just tell Claude which reps are which.

One iPhone recording: 1080p, 30 fps, Most Compatible, HDR Video off, landscape, propped steady,
whole body in frame. Stand still about 2 seconds between reps. Claude cuts it into numbered clips.
Tell Claude anything special by rep number (which leg, deliberate mistakes).
