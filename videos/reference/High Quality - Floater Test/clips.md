# High Quality - Floater Test

Filmed from the front. Arms up on the floaters. Planned combos (a 2nd turn may be extra or missing): 1-2: 2 floaters + 1 2nd + 2 pirouettes; 3-4: 2 2nd + 2 floaters + 1 2nd + 2 pirouettes; 5-6: the same with 3 floaters. Each pair is both sides.

Cut on 2026-10-13 from one iPhone recording (Floater Turns.MOV) (in full recording/), at the pauses between reps.
1080p, about 30 fps; the HDR original is tone-mapped to standard range (H.264). The camera setup at the
start and the reach for the phone at the end are left out of the clips.
