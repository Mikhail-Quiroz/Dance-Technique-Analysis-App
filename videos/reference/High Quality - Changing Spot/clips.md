# High Quality - Changing Spot

Filmed from the front. Each: 2 regular 2nd + 4 changing-spot turns + 1 regular 2nd + 2 pirouettes. 3/4 turns stay in plié with the hands together; 5/4 turns go up to relevé and down every turn, arms opening toward the new wall.

Cut on 2026-10-13 from two iPhone recordings (in full recording/), at the pauses between reps.
1080p, about 30 fps; the HDR original is tone-mapped to standard range (H.264). The camera setup at the
start and the reach for the phone at the end are left out of the clips.
