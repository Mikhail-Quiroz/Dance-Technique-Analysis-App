# Blind test

3 videos with about 10 moves each, in a mixed order (the user's plan, 2026-10-14). Film them the
way a student would: iPhone propped up, whole body in frame. One video filmed with the phone
upright helps. A few mistakes on purpose, and a move or two the app doesn't coach, are welcome.

Name the files blind 1, blind 2 and blind 3. Don't tell Claude what is in them. Write the key right
after filming (a numbered list of the moves in order, plus anything done wrong on purpose), and
send it only after Claude has posted what the app found.
